✦  The broken loop
Three lies.
Thirty seconds.

"Khaana khaaya?" Haan.
"Tabiyat theek hai?" Bilkul.
"Dawai li?" Le li.

Not because parents want to lie — but because they can't articulate "my left chest feels heavy when I climb stairs" on a WhatsApp call. The doctor is 30 km away. The trip itself is exhausting.

The patient

Symptom illiteracy in clinical language — a 68-year-old farmer says "paer mein jalan" not "intermittent claudication." That gap is where diagnoses die.

The family

18 million Indians abroad. "Surprise emergency flights" cost ₹80k–₹2L each. Almost always preventable with earlier signal.

✦  The broken loop · continued
The system is
working against everyone.

Silent non-adherence

Patients don't say "I stopped my BP meds because they made me dizzy" — they just stop. Six months later: a stroke. Costs $300B / year globally.

✦  Privacy · by architecture
Soft interface.
Hard clinical
guardrails.

The server never persists health content. The decrypted blob exists in RAM for the duration of one HTTP request, then is purged. DPDP · HIPAA · GDPR compliant by architecture, not by policy promise.

🔐  On-device private memory

Each user has a single encrypted .kutumb file. AES-256-GCM. Key wrapped by biometric via Android Keystore / iOS Secure Enclave. Never uploaded.

⚡  Rules before generation
